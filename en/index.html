FAQ

What to know before you start

If your question isn’t here, message us on WhatsApp and we’ll talk it through.

What information can I centralize in FarmIA?

Technical, production, operational and financial information: fields, seasons, maps, satellite imagery, scouting, prescriptions, documents, costs and results.

Who is the platform for?

For leadership, growers and advisors who need to work from the same picture without rebuilding information across files, messages and spreadsheets.

Does FarmIA replace the agronomist’s judgment?

No. FarmIA organizes information, connects evidence and offers suggestions. The professional reviews the context, validates it and makes the decision.

Does FarmIA work on a phone and in the field?

Yes. The platform combines the web and a mobile app to view fields and imagery and record geotagged observations during field work.

What does implementation look like?

First we get to know your operation and your priority modules. Then we organize the initial information and support your team through the rollout.

Can I see FarmIA before deciding?

Yes. We schedule a personalized demo focused on your business and on the decisions that take up most of your team’s time today.
